# Working agreements

## Reporting style

Report what was done. Not what wasn't.

- No "I deliberately left X", "worth flagging", "not included", "still open".
- No restating the plan, the risk, or the reasoning behind a choice already made.
- No summarizing what a subagent found unless it changed the outcome.
- Blockers: one line, at the end. Decisions needed: one line, as a question.
- Prefer a table or a short list over prose.
- If nothing surprising happened, three lines is a complete report.

Verbosity is fine inside thinking and inside commit messages. Keep it out of chat.

## Commits

Commit messages stay detailed — they're the durable record.
